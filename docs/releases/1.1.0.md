# Care Notes 1.1.0 — Evidence Coverage Firewall

Missing evidence must not silently become evidence of absence.

- Results lead with rule-bounded attributed accounts. Expand each claim for evidence, conflicts, coverage, blind spots and why its wording is limited.
- Coverage is computed from reviewed capabilities, subject, active intervals, outages, reporting cadence and source-clock uncertainty. An online wearable cannot establish medication observation.
- Model-denied claims default to unknown/non-observation. Stronger explicit negation requires a matching reviewed assertion, direct continuous coverage of its own source, meaningful silence and no identified opposing or unresolved evidence. No automatic medication-adherence determination.
- Deterministic falsification artifacts, explainable clarification priorities and qualitative alternate candidates preserve disagreement without fabricated probabilities.
- The API-free fictional demo includes two caregivers, a door lock, a removed watch and a camera outage. All device sources are simulated.
- Android/web share the same implementation. Existing backups and source histories are retained. Coverage corrections invalidate narrative snapshots; derived audits are recomputed on restore.
- New feature copy is English/Chinese, with explicit English fallback in other interface languages. BYOK and encrypted Android key storage are unchanged.

Validation: 149 automated tests; Worker build/runtime relay check; 12-case, 15-stage fixture replay with zero kernel failures and no model requests. Android compilation runs in CI; signed distribution is checked byte-for-byte against the web assets before publication. Two existing presentation tests now assert quarantining model prose rather than rendering it verbatim; all original source/provenance and transport checks remain.

Limitations: reviewed observation metadata is a declaration, not independent verification. Ambiguous event identity and unknown source truth remain human review tasks. Candidate timelines are local two-account alternatives, not exhaustive global histories. No real sensor integration, clinical validation, new live-model benchmark or broad physical Android acceptance is claimed.
